How to Measure Your Foot Size

1
Prepare a Sheet

Place a blank A4 paper on a flat, hard floor. Stand barefoot on it.

2
Trace Your Foot

Use a pencil to trace the outline of your foot, keeping the pen vertical.

3
Measure Length

Measure from the longest toe (usually big toe) to the heel in centimetres.

4
Find Your Size

Match your CM measurement to the chart above. When in doubt, go up half a size.

Pro Tips: Measure your feet in the afternoon when feet are slightly larger. Always measure both feet — use the larger measurement. Wear the type of socks you plan to use with the shoe.
