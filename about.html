About STEPZ

Sri Lanka's premier footwear brand — crafted with passion, delivered with pride

Our Journey

Born in Sri Lanka, Built for the World

STEPZ was founded in 2018 with a simple mission — to bring world-class footwear to Sri Lankan doorsteps at honest prices. What began as a small boutique on Galle Road, Colombo has grown into the island's most trusted online and offline shoe destination.

We partner directly with global brands including Nike, Adidas, Puma, New Balance and many more — ensuring every pair is 100% authentic, competitively priced and delivered with care.

Today, STEPZ serves over 50,000 happy customers across Sri Lanka, with three flagship stores in Colombo, Kandy and Galle, plus island-wide delivery within 2–4 business days.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

100% Authenticity

Every product we sell is sourced directly from official brand distributors and comes with authenticity verification. No fakes, ever.

Fast Island-Wide Delivery

We deliver to all 25 districts in Sri Lanka. Same-day delivery in Colombo and express 2–4 day shipping everywhere else.

Premium Quality

We carefully curate only the finest footwear from top global brands. Quality is never compromised — from product to packaging.

Easy Returns

Loved it? Great! Didn't fit? No worries — our 30-day hassle-free return and exchange policy has you covered.

Secure Payments

Shop with confidence. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and support COD, card payments and bank transfers.

Dedicated Support

Our customer care team is available 7 days a week to help you with sizing, orders, returns and anything else you need.
